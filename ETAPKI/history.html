Overview / Bach & context
The name behind the application

ETAPKI begins with taste.

ETAPKI is derived from Essay on the True Art of Playing Keyboard Instruments, C. P. E. Bach's practical guide to keyboard performance, expression, and ornamentation. The application turns that study tradition into an inspectable MIDI workspace.

Two parts · Berlin

The publication timeline.

The title is commonly given in German as Versuch über die wahre Art das Clavier zu spielen. The supplied Mitchell translation identifies the same two-part work.

1753

Part One

Published in Berlin. Its first major chapters address fingering, Manieren (ornaments/embellishments), and performance.

1762

Part Two

Published in Berlin. It turns to accompaniment and free fantasia, extending the practical keyboard method.

Later editions and additions exist, including 1759/1787 for Part One and 1797 for Part Two. ETAPKI uses the historical Part One / Part Two dates as its primary reference.

A word worth keeping

Von den Manieren

In the original German table of contents, Chapter Two is “Von den Manieren” — literally, “On the ornaments” or “On the mannerisms.” The translation renders the chapter as “Embellishments.”

the good must be correctly performed, and introduced moderately and fittingly.C. P. E. Bach, Essay on the True Art of Playing Keyboard Instruments, Chapter Two, “Embellishments,” §5, Mitchell trans., p. 79.
a prodigal use of embellishments must be avoided.C. P. E. Bach, Chapter Two, “Embellishments,” §9, Mitchell trans., p. 81.

These short quotations are presented as historical context. The supplied 1949 W. W. Norton edition is the source for the English wording; the German chapter structure is cross-checked against the historical text listing.

ETAPKI in practice

Historical principles, modern inspection.

The point is not to automate taste or replace a musician. It is to make the choice visible, reversible, and easy to hear.

◎Context

Analyzer findings identify where a musical situation occurs: repeated notes, intervals, leaps, cadences, and other target locations.

♬Choice

Compatible variants remain traceable to one of seven families, with exact, grouped, and deterministic random selection.

↔Comparison

Source, current revision, target region, notation, and piano-roll views make the before/after change inspectable.

𝄐Fermata · next version

Fermata is planned as the next additional family after its rule set and formula integration are documented and tested.
